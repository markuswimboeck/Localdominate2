Questions

How short-notice can I book?

Free slots show online up to the day before. For the same day, please call.

How much is a balayage exactly?

From €165 for shoulder-length hair. For long or very thick hair we confirm the price at the chair before we start.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before, through the link in your confirmation or by phone.

Do I need a test before colour?

For a new colour or a known allergy we do a short skin test in the salon 48 hours before.
