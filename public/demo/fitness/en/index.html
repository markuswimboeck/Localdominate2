    Questions

    What do I bring to the trial?

    Sportswear, clean indoor shoes, a towel and something to drink. We have locks for the lockers.

    How do I cancel?

    With the “Cancel contracts here” button at the bottom of every page, by email or at the desk. You get a confirmation straight away.

    Can I pause my membership?

    For illness, pregnancy or time abroad the contract is paused at no cost, with proof.

    Is it very busy in the evening?

    Busiest between 5 and 7 pm. Quieter in the morning and after 8 pm. The chart above shows a typical day.
